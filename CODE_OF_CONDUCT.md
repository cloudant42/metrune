# Code of Conduct

Metrune contributors are expected to make participation welcoming, respectful,
and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing private
information without consent. Technical disagreement is welcome when it stays
focused on the work and treats other contributors in good faith.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when conduct violates this policy.

## Reporting

Report a conduct or safety concern privately to <cloudant42@gmail.com> rather
than including private details in a public issue. Reports go to the maintainer,
who will acknowledge them and keep the reporter's identity confidential unless
the reporter agrees otherwise.

Metrune is currently maintained by one person, so a report about the maintainer
has no independent party to go to inside the project. That limitation is stated
here rather than left implied.

Security vulnerabilities follow a different path — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
